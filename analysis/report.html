Hollerau Procurement Dossier

What the purchasing data tells our buyer agent before it opens a thread: who the suppliers are, how far their asks are justified, what each one responds to, and where our leverage is real.

Summary

Seven findings for the agent

  1. Price letters ask for more than costs moved. Weighting each part's cost drivers by its index moves since the current price took effect: Brandlhuber asks +9.5 % against +2.3 %, Lindlbauer +7.0 % against +4.0 %, Platinenwerk +8.0 % against +5.5 %. Only Nordmark (+6.5 % against +6.6 %) is in line. Market news backs the counter: industrial power is 43 % below 2022 news:20260912-30, iron castings +1.8 % year on year news:20260914-31.

  2. But some of these suppliers sell below their own cost. Should-cost puts Platinenwerk at −8.4 %, Nordmark at −7.6 % and Brandlhuber at −4.8 % margin on today's price. Castings and electrical are stabilise categories, where leaving a supplier below a sustainable margin costs us in C. The right landing is near cost plus that margin, not the old price.

  3. History says asks shrink to zero. 225 past renewals asked +1.4 % on average and settled at −1.1 %; the median settlement is 0.0 % and 82 % ended flat or lower. Raw material and castings settled about 2 to 4 points below their ask.

  4. Our alternatives are mostly weak. Second-source quotes sit 8 to 21 % above the incumbent. The exceptions are the DN 65 flange (+1.9 %, sup:70240) and the coupling hub (+9.6 %). A switch threat is a bluff almost everywhere else, so the agent should not lead with it.

  5. We pay late, and suppliers notice. Hollerau pays Saalach 19 days late on average, Wälzhammer 16, Lindlbauer 13; seven suppliers sent an overdue-invoice letter. At 8 % cost of capital, 30 days earlier payment is worth about 0.67 % of price to us, often much more to a cash-short supplier.

  6. Supplier personas are written in fixed phrases. Twelve traits (cash need, spare or full capacity, open book, stale costing, approval ladder above 2 %, price freeze, hedged material, and more) appear in call notes, meeting notes, quote remarks and news with one or two fixed wordings each. A regex reads them all. Holiday, ISO, contact and packaging e-mails are noise.

  7. Five suppliers are red. Aichbauer, Kalwisz, Tarsevi, Weißacher and Vrbenský sit below 70 points. Two of them are practice escalations (P11, P12), where the goal is quality_first: PPM commitment and chargebacks matter more than price.
